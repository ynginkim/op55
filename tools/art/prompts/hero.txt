Wide 16:9 landscape frame. Extreme close-up macro photograph of a page from an 1804 hand-written orchestral manuscript in brown iron-gall ink: hand-ruled staves, dense scrawled notes, ink blots and corrections. On the title line, one name has been scratched out so violently with a quill that the paper is torn through, leaving a ragged hole with frayed fibres. Through the hole, from behind the page, a precise modern grid of thin glowing signal-red lines and a pulsing audio waveform shine out, as if the music behind the paper were made of code. The page occupies the left two thirds and dissolves into darkness; the right third is large empty pure-black negative space reserved for typography.
