3:2 landscape frame. A weathered white marble bust of a stern early-19th-century composer with wild windswept hair, seen in three-quarter view from behind so the face stays in deep shadow and is not visible. The bust wears modern matte-black over-ear studio headphones; the coiled headphone cable hangs down unplugged, its gold jack dangling in mid-air beside the plinth. The marble is lit only by coloured light from an out-of-frame screen: horizontal bands of a spectrogram projected across the stone in red, amber and deep violet. The bust sits in the right half; the left half is empty black negative space.
